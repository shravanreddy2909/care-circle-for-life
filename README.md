# Care Circle For Life

Imported website: https://carecirclefrlife.lovable.app

This project was built with [Lovable](https://lovable.dev).

**Live app**: https://care-circle-for-life.lovable.app
What it does:

Smart medicine reminders: The elder sees only their upcoming tablets and taps “I have taken it”. If a dose is missed, the elder is reminded first. After 15 minutes, all caretakers are alerted by email so someone can call.
AI report analyser: Caretakers upload scanned medical reports, and AI turns them into a simple summary. It highlights what to ask the doctor about.
AI companion for parents: Elders can ask the assistant anything, from “can I take my tablet after tea?” to questions about their reports. Caretakers can see what their parents have been asking and searching. This shows worries and concerns that a parent may never say out loud.
Wellbeing awareness: Those questions and routines act as early signals about how a parent is feeling, so the family can check in sooner.
One shared family space: Up to 3 caretakers and 2 elders, with a shared family tree and a separate dashboard for each role. It keeps everyone connected and informed.
